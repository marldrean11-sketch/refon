The idea in one line

Every phone gets a tag.

ReFon's real advantage is already in the database: a measured battery %, an 11-point checklist, a grade with written notes, and a person who reviewed the listing. Today that proof is scattered across badges that look like any other shop. This proposal turns it into one recognisable object, the ReFon Tag, and builds the identity around it.

01

Where the current site stands

The foundations are good. The copy is honest, every claim is tied to real data, and the motion and accessibility work is careful. What's missing is anything a visitor would remember after closing the tab.

Keep

  • ✓Evidence-first copy. "Battery % on every listing" and "the measured figure, not good condition" is already a brand voice. It just has no look yet.
  • ✓Real trust data. Battery %, written condition notes, the 11-point checklist and human moderation are differentiators most marketplaces can't show.
  • ✓Token discipline. One tailwind.config.js plus mirrored CSS variables makes a rebrand a contained change.
  • ✓Motion and a11y care. Reduced-motion support, focus rings and tabular numbers all carry over.

Change

  • ✕Two logos. The favicon is a navy tile with a green check circle. The header is a black pill with an outline phone. Neither is the brand.
  • ✕A borrowed look. Warm off-white, near-black, one emerald and pill buttons is the default "premium" template. It reads close to Back Market and to every Apple-inspired shop.
  • ✕One typeface for everything. Manrope for headlines, body, prices and labels gives no hierarchy of voice. Prices and specs should look measured.
  • ✕A generic hero line. "Premium phones. Smarter prices. Tested for you." could sit on any phone shop. It doesn't say what ReFon proves.
  • ✕Trust as small badges. Grade, battery and checks appear as separate chips and icons. They should read as one verdict.
02

Brand core

A student-founded Philippine marketplace for 2015–2024 phones, for buyers who want a flagship without paying flagship prices and without gambling on a stranger's "good condition".

Promise

You see the phone's real record before you pay: grade, battery, checks and marks, in the same format on every listing.

Personality

The friend who works at the repair shop. Plain-spoken, a bit nerdy about specs, on your side about price. Never hype, never luxury.

Tagline

"Know the phone before you buy it."
Alternates: "Sulit, and we can prove it." · "Every phone, on the record."

03

Logo: the tag-phone

One mark replaces both current logos. It's a phone cut like a luggage tag: the earpiece becomes the punch hole, and the screen shows a battery reading. The wordmark sets "re" in the base colour and "fon" in the brand colour, so the name reads as re + phone.

The cut corners

The chamfered top is the tag silhouette. It repeats on the Tag component, the hero panel and packaging stickers, so the shape alone says ReFon.

The battery bars

Two solid bars and one faded bar. It reads as a phone at a glance and as a measurement up close. Never animate it to "full".

Wordmark

Archivo at its widest setting, weight 900, lowercase. Lowercase is friendlier and keeps "fon" from reading as an acronym.

07

UX changes that make the brand useful

An identity only sticks if it helps people decide faster. These changes use data the API already returns.

Lead with a finder, not a carousel

Replace the auto-rotating hero carousel with one static tag and a row of budget, battery and grade chips right under it. Most buyers arrive with a budget, so let them act on it in one tap.

HeroSection.jsx · FeaturedCategoriesSection.jsx

Make battery a filter, not just a label

Add a "Battery 85%+" filter and sort. It's ReFon's most distinctive field and nobody else lets you filter on it.

CatalogFilters.jsx · catalog-filters.js

Put the full tag above the fold on the product page

Show the full tag next to the photos, before the description. Tapping a row expands it: Checks opens the 11 results, Marks opens the photos of those marks.

ListingDetailPage.jsx

Compare tags side by side

Let buyers pin up to three listings and see the tags next to each other. Two iPhone 13s at different prices only make sense when grade and battery sit side by side.

New: features/compare

Explain grading once, link it everywhere

Each stamp links to a short "How we grade" page with real photos of A, B and C wear. It answers the most common doubt about secondhand phones before checkout.

QualityCheckPage.jsx · GradingSection.jsx

Give sellers a tag of their own

Seller storefronts get a small tag too: reviewed since, phones sold, return rate. Trust in the seller and trust in the phone then look the same.

StorefrontHeader.jsx · TrustPanel.jsx

Use one loud moment, not scattered motion

Drop the smooth-scroll library, scroll progress bar and magnetic buttons. Keep a single animation: the tag swinging in when it first appears. It's lighter on low-end Android phones, which many buyers use.

SmoothScroll.jsx · ScrollProgress.jsx · use-hero-motion.js

Mobile first for real

Pin a bottom bar on the product page with the price and Add to cart, and keep the mini tag on the card visible at 360px width. Check every screen on a mid-range Android, not only on desktop.

MobileTabBar.jsx · ListingDetailPage.jsx
08

Rollout in the codebase

Because the tokens are centralised, most of the visual change happens in a few files. Ship it in four pull requests so each one is easy to review.

  1. PR 1 · Tokens and type

    Swap the palette and fonts

    Replace accent, page, panel and dark in tailwind.config.js and the mirrored --rf-* variables in index.css. Add cobalt, tag and pass. Install @fontsource-variable/archivo, instrument-sans and @fontsource/ibm-plex-mono, then point the existing display, body and mono families at them. Most components pick this up without edits.

  2. PR 2 · Logo

    One mark everywhere

    Redraw LogoGlyph and BrandMark in components/ui/BrandMark.jsx with the tag-phone and the two-tone wordmark. Replace public/favicon.svg and the manifest icons, and update theme-color and the meta copy in index.html.

  3. PR 3 · The Tag component

    Build <ReFonTag size="full | mini | stamp">

    Merge ConditionBadge, GradeBadge, BatteryHealth and PriceDisplay into one component fed by the listing object. Use it in HomeListingCard, the hero, ListingDetailPage, the cart and orders.

  4. PR 4 · Homepage and UX

    Finder hero, battery filter, copy

    Rework HeroSection and update HERO_HEADLINE in content/home-content.js. Add the battery filter, trim the motion stack, and run the Playwright suite in e2e-ui/, since some tests select hero and card elements by data-testid.
