Inspiration for the ReFon Tag identity

Borrow the idea, not the look.

Nine sites worth studying, in three groups: the refurbished-phone shops ReFon competes with, sites that turn an inspection into something people trust, and brands with a strong identity built from a few ideas. Each card says what to take, what to leave, and where it fits in ReFon.

What this means for ReFon

Everyone grades on looks only, and most promise a battery minimum instead of the real figure. ReFon's edge is the exact battery % and written notes on every phone, so put those first. Don't lead with "11-point": next to 32, 52 and 100 it looks small. Lead with "every result listed" instead. And CompAsia already sells in the Philippines, so ReFon needs a look that can't be confused with it.

Back Market

Market leader

The largest refurbished marketplace: about 1,500–2,700 vetted refurbishers, a 12-month warranty, and a trade-in service.

  • BorrowThe grade guide uses a physical yardstick: wear "invisible at 20 cm" for Excellent and at 50 cm for Good. ReFon's A/B/C notes should be just as concrete.
  • AvoidBlack, white and mint, and calm "premium" minimalism. ReFon's current site is already too close to this.
  • ReFonGradingSection.jsx, QualityCheckPage.jsx
backmarket.com · phone grades guide

Swappie

iPhone specialist

Refurbishes iPhones in-house. Publishes a battery floor and offers a "100% Brand New Battery" upgrade at checkout.

  • BorrowTreat battery as a product feature: a clear threshold, an upgrade at checkout, and a blog post explaining how to read battery health in Settings.
  • AvoidUsing two different sets of grade names on different pages. Pick A/B/C and use them everywhere.
  • ReFonBattery 85%+ filter, battery explainer on the FAQ
swappie.com · battery article

CompAsia

Local rival

A Southeast Asian refurbisher operating in 9 countries, including the Philippines, with trade-in partners such as AndroidZone.

  • BorrowTrade-in as a second front door. A "Sell your phone" path gives sellers on ReFon a reason to visit.
  • AvoidCopying its colours or grade names. Local buyers will compare the two side by side.
  • ReFonSeller onboarding, the "Sell" item in the main menu
unbox.ph · CompAsia trade-in in PH

Shelf 2 · Proof made visible

Inspection and verification patterns

These aren't phone shops. Each one turns an inspection or a check into something buyers can see and trust, which is the job of the ReFon Tag.

StockX

The physical tag

Puts a green tag on every verified item. Buyers know the tag by sight, and it has even been counterfeited.

  • BorrowOne object that means "checked", used on the product page, in the box and in buyers' photos. A QR code on the back links to the record.
  • AvoidOver-claiming. StockX dropped "100% Authentic" for "StockX Verified" during the Nike lawsuit. Say "reviewed" and "measured", not "guaranteed".
  • ReFonA printed tag in the box with a QR code to the listing's checklist
retaildive.com · StockX tag change

Carvana

Inspection report

Attaches a 150-point inspection to each certified car's listing page, along with its "top imperfections" and a 360° photo.

  • BorrowThe inspection lives on the listing, and imperfections get their own photos. ReFon's "Marks" row should open photos of the actual marks.
  • AvoidAn inspection you have to request by email. ReFon shows its checks openly.
  • ReFonListingDetailPage.jsx: the Marks row links to photos
carvana.com · certified program

iFixit

One-number score

Scores how repairable each phone is from 0 to 10, from a 100-point rubric, and shows the reasoning in each teardown.

  • BorrowA simple headline (the stamp) with the full working underneath (the checklist). Show the summary first and the detail one tap away.
  • AvoidChanging the rubric without saying so. Put a version and date on the grading guide.
  • ReFonGrade stamp → expandable checklist
ifixit.com · smartphone repairability

Shelf 3 · Identity

Brands built from a few strong ideas

These show how a small brand becomes recognisable through one typeface idea, one colour rule, or one blunt sentence.

Nothing

Type as identity

A custom dot-matrix typeface (Ndot) used for the wordmark, product names and the phone's software, inspired by 1980s IBM computers.

  • BorrowA typeface that looks like a machine readout, used for data only. That's IBM Plex Mono's job in ReFon: battery %, checks, prices.
  • AvoidDot-matrix lettering and the black, white and red palette. They are Nothing's trademarks.
  • ReFonMono for every number, Archivo for headlines
fontsinuse.com · Nothing Phone

Framework

Mission in one line

Repairable laptops. The homepage leads with "Let's fix Consumer Electronics." Brand orange is used only for actions, with mono type for specs and a parts shop filtered by compatibility.

  • BorrowA blunt mission headline, an accent colour used only for actions, and filters that match how buyers think ("fits my budget", "battery above 85%").
  • AvoidWarm off-white with orange. It's another version of the template ReFon is leaving.
  • ReFon"Know the phone before you buy it.", the finder chips
ecomm.design · Framework teardown

Gumroad

Colour with nerve

A loud 2021 redesign: cream, bubblegum pink, hard black outlines and a yellow close to #FFC900. Some people loved it, some found it distracting.

  • BorrowThe confidence to use a saturated colour as a signature. Its yellow is close to Tag Yellow, which shows how well yellow on dark navy stands out.
  • AvoidHard offset shadows and pink. Keep yellow on the tag only, or ReFon will read as neo-brutalist instead of trustworthy.
  • ReFonTag Yellow used on roughly 4% of a page
madegooddesigns.com · neobrutalism

Using this board

How to look at these sites

On each competitor's site, check

  • How many taps it takes to see battery health on a product page
  • Where the grade is explained, and whether it uses photos
  • The mobile product page on a mid-range Android phone
  • What the checkout says about warranty and returns

Where to find more

  • ecomm.design: storefront teardowns, filterable by industry
  • Search on Dribbble or Behance for inspection report UI, luggage tag label design, spec sheet typography
  • Avoid searching "refurbished phone UI", which mostly returns copies of Back Market

Grading figures come from each company's published guides and from press coverage, and some sources disagree (noted in the table). Check the live sites before quoting any figure publicly.
